Hi, I'm Megan

An avid plant lover who makes house calls.

Most plants outgrow their pots in the time we have them. Usually that's a happy problem — but sometimes a plant gets too big, too heavy, or too awkward to move and repot on your own.

That's where I come in. I'll come to your home, assess your plant in person, and move it into the right pot and soil so it can keep growing for years. I'll also share exactly how to keep it happy and healthy long after I leave.

No plant deserves to suffer just because we "didn't know." I'm here to help.

— Happy plants, happy home.

What I do

Repotting, planting & everything in between

Tell me what your plants need. I bring the tools, the soil, and years of plant-loving experience to your doorstep.

Houseplant repotting

I assess your plant and move it into the right size pot and soil. FoxFarm Ocean Forest is my standard mix; specialty soils available on request.

Container & porch planting

Annuals and container gardens for porches, patios, and yards. You pick the flowers — I bring the tools, root stimulator, and soil if you need it.

Pest control & treatment

Fighting an infestation? Mention it when you request an estimate and I'll include treatment options tailored to the pest and severity.

Pruning & propagation

Trim back overgrowth, or turn one healthy plant into several. Priced by plant, from small tidy-ups to bigger propagation projects.

Plant-care advice

Watering, light, soil, and seasonal tips so your plants stay healthy for the long haul. I want them thriving long after the repot.

How it works

Four simple steps, one happy plant

Every job starts with a free, no-pressure look in person. Here's what to expect.

1

Send an inquiry

Email what you'd like done with as many photos as possible. For houseplants, note the species (if known), age, watering schedule, light, drafts or temperature swings, and any recent stress. For containers, tell me the pot size, whether you have soil, the flowers you want, and where they'll live (full sun, under a tree, by a wall).

2

Free in-person assessment

I come out and look at the job in person — completely free of charge — and we talk through options, pot sizing, and soil together.

3

A written estimate

You'll get an estimate that's good for two weeks. Pricing reflects local plant cost, travel, and how involved the job is (some jobs need a second set of hands).

4

We schedule & plant

Pick a time and I handle the rest. Cancel free up to 24 hours before your appointment; cancellations inside 24 hours are a flat $20.
